About Me

I hold a Bachelor’s Degree in Japanese and a Master’s Degree in International Relations, and I am currently enrolled in a Master’s Degree in Translation Studies at Monash University in Melbourne, Australia. Having lived and worked across the world — in Japan, France, the USA and Australia — has given me excellent intercultural and interpersonal skills. I have extensive experience in customer-facing and language teaching roles, and have worked as a freelance translator and interpreter over the past year. I speak Italian, English, French and Japanese. I particularly enjoy transcreation and localisation projects, where creativity meets cultural awareness and linguistic expertise.
